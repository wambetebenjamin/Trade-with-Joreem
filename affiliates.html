Partner FAQ

Questions partners ask

How long does attribution last?
60 days on a first-touch basis. Use sub-IDs on your links so you can see which video, post or email produced the sale inside your dashboard.
When is commission released?
Fourteen days after purchase, which is our refund window. Approved commission is paid in the monthly run, on the 5th working day of the following month.
Can I promote with my own results?
Yes, if they are genuine and verifiable, and if you clearly state that trading involves risk and results are not typical. Fabricated screenshots, guaranteed returns and "no-loss" claims breach the partner terms and end the agreement.
Do you offer CPA deals?
Hybrid CPA plus revenue share is available from the Partner Desk tier once volumes are predictable. Talk to partnerships with your historic numbers and we will model it with you.
Can I get a discount code for my audience?
Yes — every approved partner receives a custom code (for example YOURNAME20) giving the audience 20% off, tracked automatically and reported alongside your links.
Compliance notice for partners

Partners must not present funded accounts as a salary, an investment product or a guaranteed income. All marketing must disclose that accounts are simulated, that trading involves risk and that most traders do not pass an evaluation. Breach of these rules ends the partnership and voids unpaid commission. See the Partner Terms.
