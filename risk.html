2. The Real Risks of Trading

  • Capital loss: leveraged trading of forex, CFDs, crypto or stocks can result in the loss of all or more than your invested capital. Most retail accounts lose money.
  • Volatility: markets can gap through stop-losses; spreads widen during news; liquidity can vanish.
  • Leverage cuts both ways: it magnifies gains and losses equally.
  • Crypto-specific: 24/7 trading, exchange counterparty risk, regulatory change, and extreme price swings.
  • Psychological: overtrading, revenge trading and tilt are the most common account killers.

4. Suitability

Trading may not be suitable for you. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose: not rent, not savings, not borrowed funds. If you are unsure, do not trade; build the education and paper-trade until the process is boringly consistent.

5. Your Responsibility

You are solely responsible for your trading decisions, your broker/exchange selection, and your compliance with local laws and regulations. We do not hold, manage or have access to your trading funds. We encourage the use of regulated brokers and exchanges with segregated client funds.
