FAQ

Funded account questions

Is this real money I'm trading?
No. Evaluations and funded accounts run in a simulated environment on live market prices. You trade virtual capital, and the rewards you earn are paid to you as real money once the reward conditions are met. That model is what lets us hand a 100K account to a trader who has not deposited 100K.
How much does it cost to get funded?
Evaluations start at $39 for a 5K 2 Step account. The most popular option, a 100K 2 Step, is $499 and includes a swap-free add-on option at +10%. First-time buyers can use the promo code JOREEM20 for 20% off. There is no recurring fee — you pay once per attempt.
How quickly are rewards paid?
Reward requests are reviewed within 24 hours. M-Pesa, Airtel Money and MTN MoMo payouts usually land the same working day; bank transfers take up to two working days; crypto (USDT) is typically within hours. Full details are on the rewards & payouts page.
Can I use an expert advisor, hold over the weekend or trade news?
Yes to all three. Automated strategies, weekend holding and news trading are permitted on every program. The only prohibited practices are feed abuse, tick-scalping, latency arbitrage and copying positions between accounts — the exact list is in the trading rules.
What happens if I fail an evaluation?
Nothing bad — you keep your platform knowledge and you can buy a reset at a discount or start a fresh account. We also publish the mistakes that end most challenges in this checklist, so the second attempt is usually the one that lands.
Can I hold several accounts at once?
Yes, up to $400,000 in combined simulated allocation. You may run different programs in parallel (for example a Flex and a Zero account), as long as you are not copying the same positions between them.
Which countries do you support?
Traders in 100+ countries, with a focus on East and West Africa. Local payment rails make the difference: M-Pesa and Airtel Money in Kenya and Tanzania, MTN MoMo in Ghana and Uganda, Pesapal across the region and bank transfer in Nigeria, plus crypto for anyone who prefers it.
Do you promise I will make money?
Absolutely not, and you should run from anyone who does. A funded evaluation is a paid opportunity to trade under professional conditions with a defined risk rulebook. Most traders need more than one attempt. Read our risk disclaimer before you buy anything.
