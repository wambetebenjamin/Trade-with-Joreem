Maximum loss (drawdown)

All three programs use a static maximum loss: the floor is fixed at the moment the account is issued and it never moves. That means profit you have already made is yours — it can never be "given back" by a rising drawdown line, which is the single most common complaint about trailing-drawdown evaluations.

Static floor

100K 2 Step account starts with equity of $100,000. The floor is set at $90,000 and stays there whether you are up $5,000 or flat.

Breach = account closed

Touch the floor with closed equity and the account is finished. No partial breach, no warning shots — plan your risk so you are never close.

Daily loss limit

The daily limit is designed to stop a bad morning from becoming a dead account. It resets at 00:00 UTC every day, including weekends and market holidays, and it applies to closed equity only.

  • Calculated on balance at the daily reset. If your balance is $101,000 when the clock turns, a 5% daily limit allows a drawdown to $95,950 that day.
  • Closed trades only. You can hold a position in drawdown overnight — it becomes relevant when it is closed.
  • Two ways to fail, one account. Hitting either the daily limit or the maximum loss ends the challenge.
  • Conservative is free. Most passing traders use well under half of the daily allowance.

Worked example — 100K 2 Step account

Balance at reset: $102,400 • Daily limit 5% = $5,120 • Floor for the day: $97,280. A trader risking 1% per trade ($1,024) would need five consecutive losers in one day to breach it.

Consistency rule

Consistency rules exist to stop one lucky trade from carrying a whole evaluation. We keep ours light and publish the exact threshold.

  • 2 Step Standard: your single best trading day may not exceed 40% of total profit at the moment a phase is marked clear. If it does, the phase is not failed — you keep trading until the ratio normalises.
  • 1 Step Flex: no consistency rule at all. Hit 10% and you are funded, whether it took 200 trades or one very good session.
  • Zero Challenge: no consistency rule — but rewards require 14 days of account history before the first request.
  • Weekends count as one day for crypto-only traders, so a big Saturday plus Sunday session is treated as a single trading day.

Prohibited practices

The list is short, specific and does not change. These practices either abuse the simulated feed or turn the evaluation into a lottery, which raises the cost for honest traders.

  • Latency arbitrage & feed exploitation — trading demonstrably wrong prices, including delayed-feed arbitrage.
  • Tick scalping — opening and closing positions within seconds repeatedly to exploit pricing artefacts rather than trading structure.
  • Copy trading between different people — running the same signals across unrelated accounts, including signal groups and managed accounts.
  • Account sharing or reselling — a purchased account is licensed to one verified person only.
  • Gambling behaviour on funded accounts — all-in risk on a single position with no stop, or martingale sizing that cannot survive three losses.
  • Hedging across accounts to lock losses — matching long and short positions between your own accounts to neutralise the drawdown without exposing real risk.

If a strategy sits in a grey area — grid systems, high-frequency mean reversion, news spikes — email hello@tradewithjoreem.com before you buy and we will give you a written answer.

Reward conditions

Rewards are the point of all of this, so the conditions are mechanical rather than subjective.

  • Minimum request: your share of profit must be at least $50 per request on any account size.
  • Cycle lock: requests open on your program's cycle — bi-weekly for 2 Step and Flex, weekly for Zero, plus on-demand where enabled.
  • Profit is measured after your split, and your split is shown on your dashboard before you request, so no arithmetic surprises.
  • KYC first: the first reward requires identity verification (see below). After that, payouts are friction-free.
  • Rewards do not reset your account. Balance, floor and split stay exactly as they were after the payout.
  • Refusals come in writing. If a request is declined, you receive the specific rule and the trade history that triggered it.

Rules FAQ

Can the rules change after I buy?
Your account keeps the ruleset that was published on the day you purchased. Future changes apply only to new purchases, and we publish a dated changelog on this page when anything is amended.
Is the drawdown trailing or static?
Static on all three programs. The floor is fixed when the account opens and does not follow your equity higher, so profits you have earned can never increase the amount you are allowed to lose.
Do weekends or holidays count as trading days?
A trading day counts when you close at least one position of 0.01 lots or more. Weekends count as a single day for crypto. Holidays do not count against you in any program.
Can I withdraw while a phase is still open?
No — rewards apply to funded accounts only. Evaluation accounts are for proving the edge; the payout mechanics begin the moment your Master account is issued.
What happens to an open position when I request a reward?
You must close open positions before submitting a reward request. This fixes the balance for review and avoids arguments about floating profit that has not actually been realised.
Are the rules the same on every account size?
Yes. Percentages are identical from 5K to 200K — only the cash equivalents change. Splits are identical too; the scaling plan is what raises your split as your allocation grows.
