The Story

From One Small Account to a Trading Room Full of Students

Joreem's first trade was a disaster: a leveraged FX position opened on a YouTube recommendation, closed at a loss within hours. He could have quit that day. Instead, he opened a notebook and wrote the rule that still runs everything at Trade with Joreem: protect the capital first; everything else is a detail.

Over the next several years he rebuilt his education the hard way: market structure, liquidity, position sizing, and the psychology of sitting still when your hands want to click. By year three he was consistently profitable. By year five, full-time. By year six, friends and followers started asking the same questions he'd wished someone would answer for him, clearly, and without the hype.

Trade with Joreem is the answer to that. Not a signals machine, not a lifestyle brand, just a structured, risk-first mentorship and a library of books that teach you to think like a professional trader in Forex, Crypto and Stocks.

The Method

Three Pillars, One System

01
Pillar One

Structure & Price Action

Trend, liquidity and levels read the way professionals do. You learn where the market goes, and, just as important, where it goes when it changes its mind.

02
Pillar Two

Risk & Position Sizing

The module most students skip, and the one that keeps them funded. Fixed R, max drawdown rules, and the math that turns a losing streak into a survivable one.

03
Pillar Three

Psychology & Consistency

Journaling routines, tilt control and the daily habits of traders who stay in the game for decades. The market punishes emotion; this pillar builds your immune system.
