FAQ

Mentorship Questions

How fast do I get access after paying?
Instantly. Your payment is confirmed automatically, your membership activates, and the members-only Telegram & Discord invite appears on your confirmation screen, typically within 60 seconds of payment.
I'm a complete beginner. Which plan should I start with?
Foundation (monthly) is designed for absolute beginners and lets you commit for one month at a time. The curriculum starts from zero, and you can upgrade to Annual or Elite later. You only pay the difference.
What happens if I cancel my monthly plan?
Access continues until the end of your paid period, then your membership pauses. Your dashboard history stays, and if you re-join within 90 days you pick up exactly where you left off. Annual plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee if it isn't the right fit.
Do you guarantee profits?
No. And you should run from anyone who does. We guarantee the process: a repeatable system, real feedback, and a community held to professional standards. Whether you profit depends on the risk you take and the discipline you apply. Always trade with money you can afford to lose.
Can I pay with M-Pesa / Airtel / MTN MoMo?
Yes. Mobile money checkout runs through Flutterwave (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, MTN MoMo) and Pesapal. You'll receive an approval prompt on your phone during checkout.
Is there a refund policy for digital books?
Digital products are final sale once downloaded, per our Terms of Service. If a file is corrupted or you didn't receive your download, contact us within 48 hours and we'll re-issue it immediately.
