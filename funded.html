FAQ

Funded Program Questions

Do I need experience to pass the evaluation?
No. Every funded account includes the full Trade with Joreem mentorship — the 200+ lesson curriculum, live breakdowns and the private community. You learn the system first, then prove it in the evaluation when you're ready. If you already trade, the risk module will tighten your existing edge before you fund a single cent.
What are the evaluation rules?
It depends on the plan you pick. Fast Funded is a single phase (12% target) to Master. 2-Step Pro is two phases (8% then 5%) with an 85% or 95% split. Elite Scale is two phases with a 100% split and prioritised scaling to $200K. Every plan has a 10% max static drawdown and a 4% daily cap, enforced automatically by the platform.
What is the maximum funding I can reach?
Start with a $5K, $10K, $25K, $50K or $100K challenge and scale to $200K in funding as you pass repeated reward milestones. Your profit split grows (85% → 100%) as you move through the scaling plan.
How and when do payouts work?
Request your profit split weekly, bi-weekly, monthly or on demand from the dashboard. Rewards are paid via card, PayPal, mobile money (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, MTN MoMo) and Pesapal, and standard payouts clear within 24–72 hours of approval.
What happens if I breach the daily drawdown?
Your funded account pauses automatically at the daily limit agreed at registration. You keep full dashboard access and your completion record, and you can re-enter after the pause window. The guardrails exist to protect you from a single emotional session.
Is this a guarantee of income?
No, and anyone promising that is lying to you. Trading involves substantial risk, and most challenge accounts across the industry are never funded. We guarantee a clear process, real education and transparent rules — never outcomes. Only trade with money you can afford to lose. Read our full Financial & Risk Disclaimer.
Can I pay with M-Pesa, Airtel or MTN MoMo?
Yes. Mobile money checkout runs through Flutterwave (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, MTN MoMo) and Pesapal. You'll receive an approval prompt on your phone during checkout.
Does my mentorship subscription continue after I'm funded?
It can. Funded students keep full community access for as long as they hold an active funded account, and Elite Scale students get lifetime desk access. You'll also keep one-on-one support while your account is scaling.
