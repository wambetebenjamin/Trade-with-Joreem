FAQ

Questions, Answered Straight

Do I need experience to join?
No. The Foundation plan starts from absolute zero: what a candle is, how to read structure, how to size a position, before any strategy is introduced. If you already trade, the risk and psychology modules will still save you money.
What markets are covered?
Forex (majors and key crosses), Crypto (BTC, ETH and major alts) and Stocks/Indices (US tech, S&P 500). The core system is one, price action, liquidity and risk, applied across all three.
How does the private Telegram & Discord invite work?
The moment your subscription payment clears, the checkout screen issues your members-only invite link directly to Telegram and Discord. If anything doesn't arrive, email hello@tradewithjoreem.com with your order ID and we'll re-issue it within minutes.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Credit/debit cards (via Stripe), PayPal, and mobile money in East & West Africa through Flutterwave (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, MTN MoMo) and Pesapal. All checkouts are encrypted; we never see or store your card details.
Is this a signals service? Do you promise profits?
No, and no. We teach a process you can verify yourself, and we never promise returns, because anyone who does is selling a fantasy. Members see Joreem's real live breakdowns (including the mistakes) so you can judge the quality for yourself. Trading involves real risk; only trade with money you can afford to lose.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Yes. Upgrade from Monthly to Annual or from Pro to Elite at any time from your dashboard. You only pay the difference for the remaining period, and your Telegram access stays continuous.
