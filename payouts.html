Reward cycles

You should not have to wait a quarter to be paid for a good month. Pick the rhythm that fits how you trade — there is no penalty for requesting more often.

Weekly — Zero Challenge

First request after 14 days of account history, then weekly. Best for traders who want cash flow tied to a small, tight account.

Bi-weekly — 2 Step & Flex

The standard cycle. Two weeks of trading, one request window, no cap on how many requests you make over the life of the account.

Monthly

Let profit compound inside the account and withdraw once a month. Popular with swing traders holding multi-week positions.

On demand — select accounts

Available to consistent funded traders on larger allocations, verified after your third successful reward.

  • Payment statements: every reward generates a downloadable statement with gross profit, split and net paid.
  • Sanctions screening: payouts cannot be sent to restricted jurisdictions, and identity checks must match the receiving account.
  • Data protection: documents are encrypted, access-logged and never sold. Details are in the privacy policy.

Payout FAQ

Can a reward request be refused?
Only for a reason that already existed in the published rules — a prohibited practice, an unmet minimum, or identity verification that has not been completed. Every refusal is issued in writing with the specific rule and the trades involved, and you can appeal once.
Do rewards reset my account balance?
No. Unlike some firms, a payout does not reset your balance or your drawdown floor. The account continues exactly where it was, which means your scaling progress is never wiped out by taking profit.
What is the minimum reward amount?
$50 net per request. Below that it is not economical to run a transfer, so small profits simply stay in the account until the next cycle.
How long does the very first payout take?
Allow one extra working day for KYC if you have not verified yet. After that, mobile money is typically the same day and bank transfers settle within two working days.
Do you pay in local currency?
Rewards are calculated in USD and paid in your local currency at the prevailing rate where the rail supports it (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, local bank transfer). The exchange rate and final local amount are shown on the receipt.
Can I split a reward between two methods?
Yes, once you are identity verified. You can nominate a primary rail (for example M-Pesa) and a secondary rail (for example USDT) and split each request as you prefer.
